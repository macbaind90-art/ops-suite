v2.4.8 QA NOTES

Focus of this build:
- Roster is now intended to be the employee source of truth.
- Roster add/edit archives sync to Attendance.
- Remove Employee now moves the record to Archive / Old Employees, hidden by default.
- Attendance history is preserved.

Recommended tests:
1. Add a test employee in Roster. Confirm they appear in Attendance Daily Entry and Grid.
2. Edit that employee shift/RDO/rank in Roster. Confirm Attendance updates.
3. Archive that employee from Roster. Confirm they leave normal Roster view and are marked inactive/archived in Attendance data.
4. Open Archive / Old Employees and restore the employee. Confirm they return to normal Roster and Attendance.
5. Use Roster > Sync Attendance manually. Confirm no save error.
